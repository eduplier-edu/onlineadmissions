Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GLA University Online programs.

Is GLA University Online degree valid for government & private jobs?

Yes. GLA University is recognized by the UGC and its online degrees are entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (DEB). Under UGC regulations, online degrees hold equal academic validity to on-campus degrees for all PSU exams, UPSC, state governments, and corporate recruitment.

Do I need to visit the campus in Mathura for examinations?

No. Both lectures and semester exams are conducted 100% online. You can take your examinations from the comfort of your home with a camera-enabled laptop or PC.

What is the minimum eligibility criteria for an Online MBA?

Candidates must hold a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories).

Are monthly EMI payment options available?

Yes. Universities provide convenient, zero-cost EMI plans with educational financing partners so you can pay your fees in monthly instalments without upfront financial burden.
