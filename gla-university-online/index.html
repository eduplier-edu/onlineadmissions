Frequently Asked Questions

Are GLA Online degrees entitled by UGC-DEB?

Yes. GLA University Online degree programs are entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (DEB). Degrees are recognized on par with regular on-campus qualifications for government exams (UPSC/SSC) and private corporate jobs.

How do I appear for examinations?

All term-end semester examinations are conducted 100% online through secure AI-based web proctoring. You can take them from home on a laptop or desktop computer with a functional webcam.

Can I pay my tuition fees in monthly installments?

Yes. Zero-cost and low-interest monthly EMI facilities are available through educational loan partners, making degree payments manageable.
