Where is the GLA University main campus located?

The campus is situated on NH-19 (Delhi-Mathura Road), Chaumuhan, Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, spread across a sprawling 110+ acre modern academic facility.

GLA University is recognized by UGC under Section 12B, accredited with 'A+' Grade by NAAC (3.46 CGPA), and approved by AICTE, PCI, and BCI.

Yes. GLA offers UGC-DEB entitled 100% online programs including Online MBA, MCA, BBA, BCA, and B.Com with remote proctored weekend exams.
