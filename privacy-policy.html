Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

OnlineAdmissions (“we”, “us”) operates onlineadmissions.online and provides free education counselling and admission assistance for online degree programs.

Information we collect

When you submit a form or contact us, we collect your name, mobile number, email address, program and university of interest, and basic technical data such as pages visited and the advertising source (UTM parameters).

How we use it

  • To contact you by call, WhatsApp, SMS or email about admissions and counselling.
  • To share your enquiry with the university or its authorised admission partner you showed interest in.
  • To improve our website and measure advertising performance.

Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies, the Meta Pixel and Google tags to understand how visitors use our site and to measure advertising results. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with universities/partners relevant to your enquiry and with service providers who help us run our website and communication.

Your choices

You may ask us to stop contacting you or to delete your data by writing to info@eduplier.com.
