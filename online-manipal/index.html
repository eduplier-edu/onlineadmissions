FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are Online Manipal degrees valid?

MUJ, MAHE and SMU are UGC-entitled to offer online degrees, which Online Manipal describes as equivalent to on-campus degrees. Validity can depend on the program and your purpose, so please confirm on the official UGC and university websites.

Which university should I choose: MUJ, MAHE or SMU?

It depends on your budget, goals and eligibility. MAHE programs carry higher fees and are positioned for working professionals, while MUJ and SMU offer more affordable options. Our counsellor can compare them for your profile.

What are the fees and are EMI options available?

Total program fees are shown on each course card. No-cost EMI options and scholarships may be available; your counsellor will share the latest terms.

Can I study while working?

Yes. The programs are designed for flexible learning with live and recorded sessions, so many learners study alongside a job.

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. Our one-to-one guidance is free for students. We help you compare options, shortlist the best fit and complete the application.
