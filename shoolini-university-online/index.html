Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the official Shoolini University website?

No. This is an independent admissions guidance page run by an authorized partner. Admissions, fees, exams and degrees are managed by Shoolini University. Visit shooliniuniversity.com for official information.

Are Shoolini University Online degrees valid?

Online programs are valid when the university and the specific program are entitled by UGC-DEB for the relevant session. Always verify on the official UGC-DEB website before applying.

What is the fee for the online programs?

Fees differ by program, specialization and intake, and can change. Confirm the latest fee structure with the university or request it through our counsellors.

Can I study while working?

Yes. Online programs are designed for flexible learning, although assignments and examinations still follow the university schedule.
