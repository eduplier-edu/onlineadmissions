Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details about Shoolini University online admissions.

Are online degrees from Shoolini University valid for corporate and govt jobs?

Yes. Shoolini University is recognized by the UGC and its online courses are approved by the Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB). Under UGC regulations, online degrees hold equal validity to regular on-campus programs.

Do I need to visit the campus for examinations?

No. Both your semester classes and term-end evaluations are administered online. Exams are conducted using remote proctoring technology on weekends.

What documents are required to apply?

You need scanned copies of your 10th and 12th marksheets, Bachelor's degree (for PG programs), government photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN), and a passport-size photograph.
