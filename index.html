Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. Our one-to-one guidance is free for students. We help you compare universities and programs, shortlist the best fit and complete the admission process.

Are online degrees valid?

Online degrees from UGC-entitled universities are recognised as per UGC regulations. Validity can depend on the program, the university and the purpose (jobs, higher studies, government exams), so we guide you on this before you decide. Always confirm on the official university and UGC websites.

How do you decide which program suits me?

We look at your current profile: your qualification, work experience, career goal, budget and the time you can give to studies. Based on this we suggest the university and the program that fit you.

Can I study while working?

Yes. Online programs are designed to be flexible, with recorded lectures and online sessions, so many students study alongside a job.

What documents are needed for admission?

Generally: previous marksheets and certificates, ID proof, photograph and address proof. Exact requirements vary by university and program; your counsellor will share the list.

What are the fees?

Fees differ by university and program and can change. Share your details and we will send you the latest fee structure and available payment options.
