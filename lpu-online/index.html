FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. Our one-to-one guidance is free for students. We help you compare programs, shortlist the best fit and complete the admission process.

Is the LPU Online degree valid?

Lovely Professional University is UGC-DEB entitled to offer online programs. Validity can depend on the program and your purpose (jobs, higher studies, government exams), so please confirm on the official UGC and LPU websites. We guide you before you decide.

What is the eligibility for LPU Online programs?

UG programs generally need 10+2, and PG programs need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Marks criteria and program-specific rules apply, and your counsellor shares the current requirements.

What are the fees?

Fees differ by program and may change. Share your details and we will send you the latest fee structure and payment options.

Can I study while working?

Yes. Online programs are designed to be flexible, with online sessions and recorded lectures, so many students study alongside a job.

Which documents are needed for admission?

Generally: previous marksheets and certificates, ID proof, photograph and address proof. The exact list varies by program, and your counsellor will share it.
